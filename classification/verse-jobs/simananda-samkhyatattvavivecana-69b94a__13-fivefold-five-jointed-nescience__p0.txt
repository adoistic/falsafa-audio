WORK: Sāṃkhyatattvavivecana  (slug: simananda-samkhyatattvavivecana-69b94a)

CHAPTER: 13-fivefold-five-jointed-nescience



[0] **Tats_12**

[13] [Base sūtra:] {{avidyā|Nescience}} of five joints (pañcaparvā avidyā).

[85] **Tats_12.c1**

[101] [Verse:] Darkness (tamas), delusion (moha), great delusion (mahāmoha), gloom (tāmisra), and blinding gloom (andhatāmisra)—[these are the five joints of nescience].

[266] **Tats_12.c2**

[282] [Verse:] Those two—darkness and delusion—are each eightfold, grounded in the eight prakṛtis [taken as the self]. This [great delusion] is the self-conceit of [false] self-knowledge, the pride in the lordly powers beginning with attainment (prāpti);

[532] **Tats_12.c3**

[548] [Verse:] in regard to the five fulfilled objects—the seen [and] likewise the scripturally-heard—the delusion that is the conceit "I," which is the great delusion, is tenfold.

[724] **Tats_12.c4**

[740] [Verse:] In regard to the eight lordly powers and the ten objects, [the affliction] called "gloom" (tāmisra) is reckoned eighteenfold; the despair [at their loss], called "blinding gloom" (andhatāmisra), is met with at death.

[967] **Tats_12.c5**

[983] [Verse:] This nescience of five joints is divided into sixty-two varieties.
