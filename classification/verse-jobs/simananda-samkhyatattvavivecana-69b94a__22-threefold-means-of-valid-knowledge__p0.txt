WORK: Sāṃkhyatattvavivecana  (slug: simananda-samkhyatattvavivecana-69b94a)

CHAPTER: 22-threefold-means-of-valid-knowledge



[0] **Tats_21**

[13] [Base sūtra:] The means of valid knowledge (pramāṇa) is threefold.

[81] **Tats_21.c1**

[97] [Verse:] The seen [perception], inference, and the word of a trustworthy person—this is [the threefold] means of valid knowledge. The five sense-organs [yield] perception; but [the inference of] rain [is made] by the rising of clouds—

[333] **Tats_21.c2**

[349] [Verse:] [this is] inference. "Indra [is] the king of the gods"—[such] verbal [testimony] is the means of cognition [i.e. trustworthy word].

[491] **Tats_21.c3**

[507] And in scripture (āgama) too: "Released from his own works, free of passion and hatred, possessed of knowledge, endowed with [good] conduct—such a one is to be known [as a trustworthy authority], and [the object] to be known is of like [trustworthiness]."
