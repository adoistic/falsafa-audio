WORK: Sāṃkhyatattvavivecana  (slug: simananda-samkhyatattvavivecana-69b94a)

CHAPTER: 17-the-ten-radical-topics



[0] **Tats_16**

[13] [Base sūtra:] The ten radical topics (mūlikārthas).

[66] **Tats_16.c1**

[82] [Verse:] Existence [of prakṛti], oneness, purposefulness, being-for-another's-sake, otherness, non-agency [of puruṣa], conjunction, disjunction, the plurality of {{puruṣa|Selves}}, and the persistence of the body [as] residual operation (śeṣavṛtti)—[these are the ten].

[353] **Tats_16.c2**

[369] [Verse:] This is the enumeration of the ten radical topics. From the [composite's] being-for-another's-sake, the existence of {{puruṣa|the Self}} is established [as] the cause of the difference of transformation. "The unmanifest exists, [it is] infinite, one"—and being-for-another's-sake, [discerned] through the nature of love and despair [in beings] and [through their] manifold ruin, [establishes purposefulness]; likewise the establishment of [puruṣa's] otherness from the three-constituent [prakṛti].

[877] **Tats_16.c3**

[893] [Verse:] From the inversion [of agency onto prakṛti follows] the non-agency [of puruṣa]; conjunction [is] for the sake of the puruṣa's seeing; and when the dissolution of the body has been attained, [disjunction follows] from the perception that [prakṛti's] purpose has been accomplished.

[1183] **Tats_16.c4**

[1199] [Verse:] From the difference [in beings] of [everything] from birth down to the organs, they [i.e. the puruṣas] are remembered as many. The residual operation [of the body, persisting] like the spinning of a [potter's] wheel [after the impulse has ceased]—[thus] the ten radical topics are established.

[1503] **Tats_16.c5**

[1519] [Verse:] These fifty notions [the contentments, attainments, etc.] were taught before by the [Sāṃkhya]saptati. But with the ten [radical topics and the] sixty categories, [the system] is called the "Ṣaṣṭitantra" (the Sixty-Topic Treatise).
