WORK: Sāṃkhyatattvavivecana  (slug: simananda-samkhyatattvavivecana-69b94a)

CHAPTER: 11-the-five-vital-airs



[0] **Tats_10**

[13] [Base sūtra:] The five vital airs (vāyus).

[57] **Tats_10.c1**

[73] [Verse:] Prāṇa, apāna, samāna, udāna, and vyāna—these are the five vital airs in the bodies of embodied beings.

[186] **Tats_10.c2**

[202] [Verse:] That which moves about the mouth and nose is called prāṇa, from [its] breathing (prāṇana); that which moves below the navel for the carrying-down is held to be apāna.

[379] **Tats_10.c3**

[395] [Verse:] From its location in and leading [of nourishment to] the heart it is proclaimed samāna; from its upward movement from the throat it is remembered as udāna.

[561] **Tats_10.c4**

[577] [Verse:] From its location in the joints, [and] from [its functions of] dispersing and stretching, [there is] vyāna.
