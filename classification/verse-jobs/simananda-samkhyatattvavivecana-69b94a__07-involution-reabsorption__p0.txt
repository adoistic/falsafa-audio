CHAPTER: 07-involution-reabsorption



[0] **Tats_6**

[12] [Base sūtra:] {{saṃcara / pratisaṃcara|Involution}} (pratisaṃcara).

[81] **Tats_6.c1**

[96] [Verse:] In just the reverse order they dissolve: the pentad of [gross] elements [dissolves] into the subtle elements; the subtle elements and the organs dissolve into the I-maker;

[278] **Tats_6.c2**

[293] [Verse:] then the I-maker [dissolves] into the intellect, and the intellect into that named the unmanifest; [but] the unmanifest is dissolved into nothing. This is remembered as "involution" (pratisaṃcara).
